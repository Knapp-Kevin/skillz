# Contract review map (employer/PBM/administrator)

Use as a **question library**, not an assertion that any provision is unlawful or a universal market standard. The agreement's definitions, role, plan type, jurisdiction, and amendments control. Do not turn an absent item into a negative finding until the document inventory is complete.

## Source precedence and definitions
- Identify parties and affiliates; business model; sponsor/plan versus administrator obligations; relevant policy/renewal/termination windows.
- Capture master agreement, statements of work, pharmacy schedules, guarantee/settlement exhibits, data use and confidentiality terms, amendments, supersession, and incorporation by reference.
- Build a **precedence graph** using only stated order-of-precedence clauses and effective dates; where unclear record ambiguity.
- Extract exactly defined \`rebate\`, \`discount\`, \`fee\`, \`administrative payment\`, \`aggregate guarantee\`, \`brand\`, \`generic\`, \`specialty\`, \`biosimilar\`, \`ingredient cost\`, \`dispensing fee\`, \`net cost\`, \`spread\`, and \`affiliate\` where present. No definition is inferred from everyday English.

## Compensation and incentives
| Theme | What to compare | Data required to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturer funds | Named rebate pass-through vs exclusions for service/admin/data fees and affiliates; gross vs net; timing and true-up | Contracts, remittance schedules, manufacturer disclosures, credits |
| Pharmacy payments | Sponsor charge, pharmacy reimbursement, spread/markup terms, guarantees, reconciliation | Per-claim plan charge and pharmacy settlement on same claims |
| Administrative fees | Per-paid-claim or per-member rate, included/excluded services, invoices, adjustment rules | Covered period, denominator, invoices, rate schedule |
| Specialty/formulary | Drug classification authority, specialty pharmacy relationships, preferred drugs, rebate incentives | Definitions, formulary/version, applicable claim-level fields |
| Guarantees | Price/discount/rebate guarantee basis, aggregation, exclusions, penalties, cure/true-up windows | Eligible universe, contract formula, certification, offsets |
| Affiliates | Ownership, related-party transactions, pass-through exceptions and disclosure rights | Affiliate services, settlements, invoices, remuneration details |
| Member incentives | Cost sharing, copay accumulator/maximizer, steering, access restrictions if in contract | Relevant provisions, plan documents and appropriate context |
| Exit/recourse | Termination, renewal, change-of-control, audit rights, time limits, access to books, dispute resolution | Governing provisions, stated timelines, signed amendments |

## Reasoning checks
1. Quote only short minimum excerpts in external reports and cite source ID, page, section, version. Store larger exact text in the authorized private source only.
2. Distinguish **economic opacity** (a payment excluded by definition) from **breach** (a contractual promise contradicted by transaction evidence). Some arrangements may lawfully permit retained compensation; the question is whether that is disclosed and acceptable to the plan.
3. Do not automatically interpret the difference between sponsor charge and pharmacy reimbursement as hidden misconduct. The difference can include agreed margins, taxes, discounts, timing and different data bases.
4. No "100% rebate pass-through" finding without reading defined \`rebates\`, exclusions, aggregation, credited time and affiliate treatment.
5. Model the opposing interpretation: a later specific exhibit, explicit carve-out, or offset could resolve a perceived conflict. Preserve it in the finding.
6. If identifying healthcare data is encountered, minimize or stop as prescribed in the core skill. Never repeat person-level rows in a public report.

## Regulatory source strategy, not a permanent compliance table

Before making a legal claim, retrieve the current primary source and confirm: (a) effective and applicability date, (b) named entity and plan type, (c) geographic and contractual scope, and (d) amended/stayed status. The following are **navigation starting points**, not permanent factual authorities:

- U.S. Department of Labor, PBM fee-disclosure final rule and fact sheet: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/resource-center/fact-sheets/pharmacy-benefit-manager-fee-disclosure-final-rule
- Official Federal Register 91 FR 69092 (Nov. 2, 2026): https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/FR-2026-11-02/2026-22306 . As checked 2026-11-06, rule effective 2027-01-04 and applies to plan years beginning on/after 2027-07-01 for relevant covered arrangements; **do not apply retroactively or to all plans**.
- HHS HIPAA de-identification guidance: https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html . Verify role and method, do not label a file de-identified simply because names were removed.
- CMS Transparency in Coverage rules: https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/transparency-coverage-final-rules-cms-9882-f . Verify applicable plan, file availability, effective date and methodology before using rates as benchmarks.

Other governing law and contractual duties may differ by jurisdiction. This skill does not contain an exhaustive compliance list.
