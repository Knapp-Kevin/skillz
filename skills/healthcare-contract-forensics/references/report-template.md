# Mandatory review report shape

Use the following fields exactly or with equivalent human-readable labels. A template is not evidence: populate only from reviewed authorized sources.

## Summary
- **Review date and scope / reviewer role / authority:** ...
- **Decision takeaway (3–5 sentences):** State most consequential verified observations, top uncertainties and next decision.
- **Document inventory:** `ID | name | exact effective version | pages/rows available | completeness / OCR state | confidentiality handling`
- **Exclusions:** Jurisdiction, missing schedules, unavailable transactions, regulatory topics, etc.

## Findings register
| ID | Priority | Classification | Question/observation | Source ID / precise location | Counter-reading / evidence against | Value basis | Confidence | Next verification owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `HCF-001`, `HCF-002` etc. Priority is a triage judgment (**high/medium/low**) based on possible impact and urgency, **not** a legal or fraud determination. `Value basis` is one of `verified arithmetic amount`, `modeled scenario with inputs`, or `not quantified`. `Confidence` refers to confidence in **that limited claim**, not recovery or malfeasance.

## Reconciliation workpapers
| Workpaper | Governing clause | Data rows | Included/excluded count | Formula | Expected | Observed | Variance | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

Do not include this section as if measured when no authorized data exists; state `NOT PERFORMED` with exact missing items.

## Decision and requests
1. **Highest-value document/data requests:** Ask for precise schedules, settlement reports, adjustment memos, audit access, definitions, and version/effective-date confirmations.
2. **Negotiation questions (not pre-approved redlines):** Cite the specific uncertainty and request clarification or different economics; route drafting to qualified counsel.
3. **Regulatory questions:** Include jurisdiction, intended plan year, *source fetched on date*, scope and effective/applicability dates; do not assert compliance without current primary authority and legal review.
4. **Privacy and authority note:** What authorized material was inspected, minimization and egress constraints, whether PHI/PII was excluded.
5. **Completion receipt:** `REVIEWED`, `PARTIALLY REVIEWED`, or `BLOCKED`; list exact missing elements, calculations not performed and whether any actual external host test was run (normally none).

**No vendor-contact messages, legal notices, direct system updates or public accusations are issued by this passive skill.** Those are separately authorized actions.
