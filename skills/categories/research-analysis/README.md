# Research & Analysis

Skills for gathering evidence, comparing options, checking claims, and interpreting research.

- [Technology Change Intelligence](../../authored/technology-change-intelligence/SKILL.md) — interest-first research and source-grounded updates across any technology; 17 bundled specialist profiles, optional portable subscriber preference drafts, and ad-hoc emerging-topic discovery without automatic scheduling.
- [Research Question Dossier](../../authored/research-question-dossier/SKILL.md) — independent evidence perspectives and adversarial challenge (replaces historical `deep-dive`).
- [Claim Evidence Adjudicator](../../authored/claim-evidence-adjudicator/SKILL.md) — test factual claims with primary-source tracing, contradiction records and explicit uncertainty (replaces `fact-check`).
- [Research Methods Appraiser](../../authored/research-methods-appraiser/SKILL.md) — critique research methods, evidence and reproducibility with a practitioner digest (replaces `paper-digest`).

Use [Decision Tradeoff Workbench](../../authored/decision-tradeoff-workbench/SKILL.md) for source-grounded comparisons. The [original Compare](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/tree/27a1b1ac732b1b5752cc50527e3ec211d7ca311c/skills/compare) remains archived for historical review, not installation.
