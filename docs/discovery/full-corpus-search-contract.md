# Full-corpus capability discovery contract

**Owner correction: 2026-11-06 (issue #714).** The 1,039-entry governed review-name index is **a review-ledger lookup, not the size of the Skillz corpus and never a stop condition**. Selecting from those names alone is incomplete capability discovery. This rule controls every skill search, candidate gap claim, and decision to CREATE, ADAPT, or recommend a skill.

## Required discovery procedure for every capability search

1. **Express the outcome independently of a filename.** Define capability domain, relevant subdomains, adjacent domains, users, authority/sensitivity, expected inputs, output and acceptance tests. Synonyms matter: for healthcare contract audits, search contract review, payer, benefits, PBM, pharmacy, medical billing, claim reconciliation, reimbursement, rebates, audit rights, legal risk, insurance, document extraction and numerical verification.
2. **Browse the canonical 33-domain capability directory** (\`registry/capability-directory.json\`) and the linked evidence, then search \`registry/governed-review-name-index.json\` for exact-reviewed leads. These are *seed sources*, not the destination.
3. **Expand across all accessible indexed discovery source families rather than stopping at the seed:** first-party \`skills/\`, governed provenance/verification companions, Q-series curation ledgers, G1–G3 source-family method alternatives and pinned package identities under \`registry/discovery/gitskills-*\`, the four-source pinned \`registry/discovery/dynamic-four-source-frontier-20261101.json\` and its per-source/chunked leads, \`registry/discovery/agentskillexchange-*\`, \`registry/discovery/github-awesome-copilot-*\`, \`registry/discovery/j4flmao-*\`, and newer relevant published source/pin ledgers. Each is a search lane, not a priority publisher. Consult \`registry/sources.yaml\` for provenance only after capability matching.
4. **Search by mechanism as well as name**, including nearby domains such as Law + Medicine + Finance + Research + Analytics + Business. Use available indexes/manifests to search broadly and resolve promising identities to exact upstream source trees. Do not attempt to claim that an excerpt, filename, model summary or root README is a full package review.
5. **Compare strongest candidates with actual current source evidence.** For each, identify source/publisher, exact pin/path/tree, completeness (including referenced files and executable helpers), workflow fit, adversarial and privacy hazards, license and copying permissions, known revisions, and current semantic/security/behavioral status. Distinguish \`FIND\` (name lead), \`READ\` (actual package), \`REVIEW\` (quality adjudication), and \`APPROVE\` (authorized use). A lead is never automatically install-approved.
6. **Apply the license gate before material reuse.** Independently developed general procedures may be written without copying protected expression. Copying, adapting, bundling, publishing and redistributing third-party materials require actual applicable source/package/nested license evidence and notice conditions; mirrors and autogenerated candidates need original provenance. \`none\` or absent metadata means **license not established**, not public domain or free reuse. Unknown/contradictory rights => no import; record a link-only or independently authored approach.
7. **Report breadth truthfully.** Record searched/indexed source families, query terms, inspected counts when measurable, dates/snapshot pins, candidates actually read, exclusions, inaccessible or too-large shards, unresolved license flags, and any source family *not covered*. Never claim the whole corpus was inspected when only indexed summaries or one family was searched. Describe gaps as “no suitable match in inspected surfaces” until broader lanes are covered.
8. **Decide deliberately** among USE EXISTING, ADAPT/COMPOSE, CREATE, DYNAMIC/CHECKLIST or NO CHANGE based on need and evidence, not the initial 1,039 names. If fresh work is requested, a remaining unsearched family must appear as an explicit **discovery limitation**, not silently disappear behind a definitive novelty claim.

**No full-corpus guarantee:** Even a complete sweep of today's pinned discovery manifests only establishes coverage of *those exact snapshots*. It does not prove that no relevant skill exists in the open ecosystem or that 61,326 records are independently reviewed. Current inventories change; use latest actual source receipts and mark historical snapshots as such.
