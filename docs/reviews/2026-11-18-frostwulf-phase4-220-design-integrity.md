## Quality-standard caveat: bounded score ceiling

The seven-dimension paired quality score has an upper bound of 100%. Consequently an aggregate `new / old >= 1.5` ratio is **mathematically impossible** if an old score already exceeds 66⅔% of that maximum. A proposed 1.5× quality target must therefore report baseline headroom and failure modes; refusing to delete a functionally equivalent predecessor solely because an impossible bounded-score ratio cannot be attained would be a defective decision rule. Distinguish any measured improvement in reliability, critical failures or comparable task efficiency as **separate preregistered metrics**, without claiming one stands in for another.

Critical output loss and safety regressions remain non-compensatory failures; improvement is a separate goal with explicit owner disposition.
