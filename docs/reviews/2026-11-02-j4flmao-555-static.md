## Macro findings

- **Two generations.** The v1.0.0 packages are long multi-reference guides (a SKILL.md plus `*-fundamentals`/`*-advanced` references) and mostly score 10–14. The v2.0.0 single-file packages are generally current and account for most of the 106 threshold signals. These cover quantum computing, R&D, embedded systems, observability, git workflow, management, SEO and system design.
- **Accuracy.** Defects recur across the corpus:
  - invented or stale APIs, CRDs and flags;
  - references that contradict their own SKILL.md;
  - arithmetic and scale errors in product and planning references, such as sample sizes about 10x off and a RICE score 1000x too high;
  - references corrupted by shell-interpolation damage or leftover PowerShell `Set-Content` fragments;
  - unlisted or orphaned reference files.
- **Boilerplate.** Some SKILL.md files end with about 120 lines of unrelated Observer/STRIDE/deployment-checklist text. Four are rejected for `capability-content-mismatch` where that text dominates.
- **Style directive.** Many packages say "No preamble… why use many token when few do trick". It is recorded as a response-style finding, not an `embedded-agent-instruction` hard fail.
- **Authority.** 37 procedures have the agent itself act without per-action confirmation. The actions are:
  - provisioning or destroying infrastructure (Pulumi `up`/`destroy --yes`, cloud-provider skills, sandbox execution);
  - initialising or unsealing Vault and changing its policies;
  - deploying to production or app stores (serverless, mobile deployment, Ionic/Capacitor);
  - broadcasting chain transactions (web3-dapp);
  - running fault injection or load against systems (both chaos-engineering procedures, load testing);
  - automatic rollback and promotion (smoke testing);
  - changing identity and access (identity-provider).
- **Harmonized authority rule.** Some packages state end-state completion criteria ("X deployed", "migrations applied", "published to broker") or give command recipes for the user's own CI, while their declared output is configuration or design text. These score authority 1 with a note, not a hard fail. Six second-pass rejects were reverted under this rule: secrets-management, both contract-testing skills, drizzle, prisma and kubernetes-for-data. Each reversal is listed with its original reason in `reconciliation_overrides`. Adapting any of these must add explicit confirmation first.
- **Secrets.**
  - Nine `secret-logging` rejects cover unmasked tokens or connection strings written to CI outputs, Authorization headers logged by production clients, and printed break-glass codes.
  - Placeholder `user:pass@` URIs in examples are notes only.
  - `relational-database` is rejected for `credential-in-url` because it directs cloud secret keys into SQL URL query strings.
- **Security material.**
  - `management/pentesting` remains REFERENCE-ONLY because its methodology requires signed authorization. Its threshold signal is withdrawn because it bundles runnable credential-dumping, persistence and exfiltration automation.
  - The red-team and reverse-engineering personas are rejected because their only misuse control is a disclaimer.
- **Licensing.** No license conflicts were found. All packages fall under the root MIT terms.

## Quality controls

- **First pass.** 44 parallel read-only reviewers worked on disjoint exact package lists with one shared brief and read the package files at the pin.
- **Integration checks.** These covered exact blob/tree identity against the manifest, taxonomy membership (2 corrected), agreement between scores and rationale prefixes, and consistency between hard fails and dispositions.
- **Second pass.** Nine independent read-only workers re-adjudicated 292 REFERENCE-ONLY candidates that had authority, privacy, missing-file or rights notes. They produced 2 rejects (devops/chaos-engineering, data-pipeline-cicd) and 1 threshold withdrawal. Integration then reverted 6 proposed rejects under the harmonized rule above.
- **Overrides.** 22 integration overrides in total: 19 floor moves, 2 second-pass rejects and 1 withdrawal.
- **Coverage honesty.** Nine records state that files over 60 KB were characterised by outline, pattern scans and sampled sections rather than read line by line.
