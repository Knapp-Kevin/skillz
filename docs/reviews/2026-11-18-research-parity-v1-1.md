## Functional outcomes

**Research Methods Appraiser:** predecessor supplied a readable paper
digest: narrow claim versus abstract, method in plain language,
admitted versus unadmitted limitations, replication evidence and
operator relevance verdict. Replacement v1.0 improved methodology and
adoption controls, but did not guarantee that accessible digest form.
The v1.1 instruction explicitly restores all five report purposes,
while preserving version pins, controlled baselines, cost budget and
experimental applicability. Inaccessible full text remains
ABSTRACT_ONLY, and no scoped source search is misrepresented as
a universal finding about absent replications.

**Research Question Dossier:** predecessor called for adversarial
refutation attempts against the claims on which the conclusion
depends, across independent primary, practitioner, analytical
and critical evidence. Replacement v1.0 allowed an effective evidence
plan but did not require individual challenge status for all central
claims or a concise executive answer with matched confidence.
The v1.1 instruction now records attempted contradiction and
NOT_TESTED on each load-bearing assertion, explicit coverage across
independent source classes, as-of freshness review and a compact
conclusion tied to the actually consulted evidence.
