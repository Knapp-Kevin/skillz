## Material source and rights defect

Three distinct authored skills declared first-party MIT and pointed
`license_evidence_path` at repository root `LICENSE`, yet the license
was **not present** in their complete downloadable package tree.
Standalone offline copies would omit the license's own requirement
to include the copyright and permission notice. Their root-license
evidence was valid for an in-repository checkout, not for distribution
of only the skill folder. This is a real package-completeness defect,
not a cosmetic preference.
