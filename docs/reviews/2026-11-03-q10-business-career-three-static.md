## Most useful finding: evidence before career claims

Evidence Miner does something worth learning from. It starts from traceable work artifacts, labels what those artifacts actually demonstrate, and asks the person to confirm impact rather than turning commit volume into fictional business results. That is more valuable than another resume adjective improver.

However, its privacy contract says results stay local while its command list includes GitHub CLI requests, potentially to remote private repositories. Its sourcing step calls for immediate tool execution after selecting repository paths, with no separate connected-account/egress gate. It hands off to a sibling STAR Story Extractor and local `story-bank/` workspace. Finally, both career skills mandate an unrelated product promotion in the final output. Those are real unchanged-use limitations, not cosmetic objections. The STAR sibling is present at blob `1d39ab044a71a827e33a2ca7aff8169a30e7727b`, and the CV sibling at `eb061a9e1bdd28b2efadb6c8da9941eb3bb09bb4`, at the reviewed pin. Both were inspected to understand the dependency, **not** counted as additional reviewed/admitted skills.

## ATS detection: useful identification, unproven parsing prescriptions

ATS Detective has a stronger source-evidence method than guessing the platform from a company name. It checks the posting URL, then optionally a handful of public board APIs, and distinguishes a mismatched company, an empty board, and an unsupported probe. These are conceptually useful controls.

Its later guidance makes categorical claims about which vendors need DOCX or parse numeric dates badly, and which understand PDF/two-column layouts. We did **not** verify these claims against current vendors or representative application uploads. An agent should not tell applicants they are established facts from this source alone. External board responses also remain untrusted data, not instructions.

## Business method: useful structure, insufficient evidence handling

Strategy Articulation provides a clear sequence: vision, mission, objectives, priorities and actions. It does not ask where real financial/market figures come from, distinguish proposed from approved targets, or resolve missing responsible owners. A planning worksheet is not a board resolution. The conceptual outline is fair prior art, but its standalone method is not sufficiently differentiated for the featured shelf.

## Safety and license boundaries

Both upstream roots have **complete MIT license text at the exact pins**, not merely repository badges. For source copying or a substantial adaptation, maintain the upstream copyright and permission notice. No third-party content was included in these two new skillz review records. The two career skills also contain third-party product branding; MIT does not imply a right to use someone else's trademarks in a new product.

Static thought experiments were used to challenge three failure families: fabricated goals/metrics; private cross-account or GitHub API reads without clear authority; and unsupported ATS parsing assumptions. They were **not executed**, so `security_scan=NOT_RUN` and `behavioral_validation=NOT_RUN` stay unchanged.
