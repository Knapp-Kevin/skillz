## Measured evidence-retrieval throughput (paired, exact outputs)

We measured the **source GitHub evidence collection stage only**, fetching the same three exact SKILL.md blobs plus their three complete package-tree objects with SHA checks. We reversed the order to reduce warm-cache/order bias.

| Trial | Order | Six sequential fetch+checks | Six concurrent fetch+checks | Speedup |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Serial then parallel | 2,723 ms | 801 ms | 3.40× |
| 2 | Parallel then serial | 3,531 ms | 1,293 ms | 2.73× |

Combined elapsed times: 6,254 ms sequential versus 2,094 ms parallel; measured **2.99× faster evidence collection** for this 3-package case. Both variants returned identical exact fingerprints; no source executed, private connector used, installed dependency, or side effect. Timing is host/network dependent and only two small trials. **This does not measure or establish 2.99× faster semantic reviewers, better decision quality, or corpus-wide throughput.** The benefit demonstrated is less reviewer waiting during evidence acquisition; rubric quality gates remained identical. Further end-to-end reviewer-effort benchmarking belongs in continuous #599.
