## Functional outcome analysis

**Business Presence Observatory:** v1.0 added stronger customer
journey and listing correctness evaluation but lacked the old
monthly **portfolio-level operations dashboard**, explicit
reachability recheck before DOWN, and applicable four-to-six-week
seasonal lookahead. V1.1 restores a bounded portfolio matrix,
dated actual fetch coverage, owner-confirmed contact/listing
comparison, explicit seasonal relevance, and prioritized
ready-to-review copy/change proposals. It does not submit customer
forms or make live CMS or directory edits.

**Professional Market Evidence Scout:** v1.0 improved posting
deduplication and compensation-unit correctness but did not
guarantee the previous role-cluster counts, prior-report trend
comparison guard or concise ahead/gap positioning view. V1.1
restores a cluster-specific market view, sanitized outbound
search inputs, evidence-based shift signals only when a comparable
approved baseline exists, and two or three practical positioning
actions. No live vacancy, salary or personalized profile evidence
has been verified in this source-only review.
