## Cross-family synthesis and omissions

1. **Preview approval is not disclosure approval.** Adobe has real pilot/sample checkpoints, but those happen after upload/processing may already have occurred. Egress capability and tool availability must not be used as user consent.
2. **Presigned asset URLs need confidential handling.** Adobe's S3/Creative Cloud URLs may grant access to images/videos. The source frequently displays direct URLs or stages local media; a host adaptation needs recipient scoping, redaction, expiry/retention handling, and explicit sensitive-content transfer approval.
3. **Destructive-only gates miss ordinary writes.** Airtable's CLI separates `write` from `destructive` but gates only the latter. Creation and edits are persistent side effects even when non-destructive.
4. **Strong mechanisms worth retaining:** Adobe one-sample-before-batch, preset-plan consistency, explicit unsupported-tool fallbacks and Quick Cut re-upload limitation; Airtable field-ID/choice-ID filters, date-timezone objects and interface-permission distinctions.
5. **No denominator omission:** the two pinned plugin skill directories contain exactly 6 + 3 canonical direct SKILL.md manifests. No additional nested skill or symlink was counted. The lack of plugin README/license files is a source-documentation/redistribution gap, not a reason to invent packages.
6. **No portability laundering:** these are service-specific skills; their `agents/openai.yaml` files and Adobe icons are upstream-owned package assets, not executable `skillz` infrastructure.
