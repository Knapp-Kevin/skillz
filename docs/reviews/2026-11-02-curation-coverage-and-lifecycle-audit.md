### Four rejected-status spelling variants fixed for presentation, not erased

The original review-path ledger contains four terminal j4flmao records labeled literally `REJECT unchanged` rather than `REJECT`. They refer to client-engine level-streaming, anti-cheat-server, matchmaking-lobby and scaling-architecture. The ledger also contains 3,600 `REJECT` records. The prior public summary **3,600 REJECT** undercounted **four genuinely rejected** records despite correctly reporting **4,809 terminal decisions**. For the dashboard, normalize these two literal spellings to one unchanged-use rejection state: **3,604**. The original record identities, scores and labels are retained unchanged for auditability.

The source's 3 DEFER records and the non-skill contributor template are NOT terminal accepted/rejected skill reviews. They remain excluded from the 4,809 disposition denominator.

### Why cumulative accepted/adapted/promoted are deliberately not numeric yet

The intake policy distinguishes **ADMIT**, **ADAPT/EXTRACT**, **REFERENCE-ONLY** and **REJECT**; `registry/candidates.yaml` contains historical July `adopted` labels (7), but those do not establish exact-version admission, current package-security state, actual successful adaptation, or live installation. The maintained 44 first-party skills are existing governed references, not a demonstrated 44 fresh promotions. The 995 third-party companions have heterogeneous semantic statuses. Any invented accepted or adapted total would make the publication **less** trustworthy.

The baseline must therefore display **UNRECONCILED** until terminal, exact-tree, source-and-rights-bound admission/adaptation/promotion events are audited. A future counted event must identify decision type, source revision and tree, substantive changes and output target (for adaptation), license/attribution, gate results, authorizing owner where applicable, decision date, and any superseded event. An inferred mechanism, static threshold or the word 'adopted' in an old intake queue is not a new event.

## Domain coverage is not category completeness

The canonical [33-domain / 247-subdomain map](../../registry/capability-directory.json) is incomplete. The [subdomain-level audit](../../registry/discovery/capability-coverage-audit-20261102.json) crosswalks the canonical map with the newer 550 Q1–Q4 reviewed candidates:

- **53 subdomains** have a direct governed-review pointer; these may include rejected entries and do not imply usable installs.
- **201 subdomains** have at least a governed pointer OR a REFERENCE-ONLY candidate pointer.
- **210 subdomains** have any governed/source-vetted pointer, including rejects; **37** have no individual reviewed/candidate pointer. The **nine** additional without a non-rejected pointer have rejected-only leads.
- Existing older four-source candidate example links cover 158 subdomains, with 1,298 link *instances*. Newly routed Q1–Q4 candidate reviews cover 135 subdomains with 550 instances; overlaps may repeat subdomain and source references.
- Source count and independent-method count **per subdomain are unknown**. A subdomain with one example, or even many identical copies, is not necessarily adequately represented.

Priority current gaps are **music-composition (8/8 with no individual link)**, illustration (5/6), 3D/CAD (2/9), audio/voice (3/8), video/film (4/9), communication (2/7), and selected robotics/game topics. The 29 music entrypoints independently recovered from held #590 are discovery identities, **not yet fully reviewed mechanisms**. This is a clear queue for selective promotion of evidence, not grounds to erase the valuable discovery archive.

## Continuing coverage rule

For every canonical and provisional subdomain, preserve a passive status: not explored; source leads only; identity verified; complete source static candidate inspected; governed semantic verified; and separately security and behavioral evidence. Seek **multiple independent original-method alternatives** where they exist and retain licensing/NOTICE obligations, useful rejected findings and source diversity. Make no blanket completion claim before the owner can see actual ranked alternatives and their stage/rights.

**Next practical actions:** (1) address 37 unlinked existing subdomains and the 9 additional rejected-only subdomains using source-backed research; (2) deep-review music, illustration, audio, 3D, robotics and other weakest domains; (3) reconcile 25 provisional domain tracks into canonical taxonomy only with actual evidence; (4) establish a future audit-backed event ledger for admitted/adapted/promoted outcomes without retrospectively guessing counts; (5) preserve separate static review, security, behavior and distribution approval gates.

Nothing here installs, executes, copies third-party source content, automates discovery, authorizes robot motions, runs GitHub Actions or changes a private visitor's skills.
