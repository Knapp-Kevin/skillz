## Consumer adoption and usage
A skill being available, reviewed, or selected does **not** mean it has been installed or used in another repository. Adoption evidence requires an observed consuming project/host, skill identifier, exact version/revision, evidence date, and permission to publish the fact. Unknown adoption remains **unknown**, not zero and not an inferred success story. See [#405](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/405).
