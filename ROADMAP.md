# Roadmap

`skillz` is an entirely passive AI-agent skill knowledge resource. Its structural architecture is established; **corpus quality, security qualification, and issue/PR lifecycle closure are ongoing work**, not evidence that a repository runtime is needed.

## Current milestone: governed curation and acceptance

Use [CURATION_QUEUE.md](CURATION_QUEUE.md) and [docs/SYSTEM_STATE.md](docs/SYSTEM_STATE.md) for live source-family denominators, decisions, and active frontiers. Do not treat historical issue descriptions or old tranche counters as current.

Priorities:

1. Resolve actionable open PR/issue debt. Merge only correct, authorized, fully qualified work; preserve exact blockers for drafts.
2. Complete and refresh admitted-source exact-version provenance and semantic reviews from the live eligible denominator. Preserve decisive VERIFIED, REJECTED, and RETIRED states without treating all as approved for unchanged use.
3. Obtain qualifying **external-host** package-security evidence for applicable exact packages, particularly first-party repairs and new admissions. Track the corpus backfill under [#402](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/402).
4. Obtain representative **actually executed external behavioral/adversarial evidence** when claiming VALIDATED. Static scenarios remain semantic-review evidence, not observed outcomes.
5. Resolve governed candidate intake with decisive source-vetting and licensing outcomes; prioritize differentiated mechanisms over duplicate wrappers.
6. Keep the five public accounting surfaces in agreement after material tranches: README, SYSTEM_STATE, CURATION_QUEUE, INDEX, and index.json.

Current first-party authority and pulse repairs are tracked in [#415](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/415), [#478](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/478), [#481](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/481), and [#483](https://github.com/Knapp-Kevin/skillz/issues/483). Their draft implementations are not shipped merely because GitHub reports them mergeable.

## Corpus posture

The current published reference surface includes:

- **44 first-party user-facing skills** under `skills/`, with 44/44 provenance companions;
- **12 intact pinned third-party corpora** under `skills/sources/<source-id>/`, contributing to the broad 500+ reference surface;
- **826 persisted third-party exact-version semantic review companions**, with individual dispositions and limits.

These figures describe inventory and persisted evidence, **not** universal security approval or behavioral validation. Exact source-family denominators and current decisions belong to the living curation ledger.

## Evaluation and architecture boundaries

The repository stores instructions, procedures, source material, provenance, licensing context, controlled tags, static semantic assessments, security evidence, and catalog snapshots. An authorized **external host** performs discovery, review, scanning, behavioral exercises, installation, and other active work.

`skillz` owns **no runtime, scripts, tests, CI workflows, schedulers, monitors, crawlers, installers, synchronizers, preflight processes, generators, background services, vector databases, autonomous observers, or personalization services**. Pinned upstream skill packages may contain their own scripts, templates, fixtures, references, or other components; preserve those intact as source-owned evidence.

Semantic `verified` is not `validated`; behavioral `validated` is not a package-security pass. Security `not-run`, `failed`, `incomplete`, or `stale` cannot be waved away by reputation or a good semantic score. A host must obtain separate authority for consequential mutations, private-data access, communications, scheduling, and persistent state.

## Durable design rules

1. Compare before creation; user fit before reuse.
2. Prefer the smallest coherent fitted set over maximum skill count.
3. Source availability, popularity, and admission are not individual quality or security evidence.
4. Preserve exact-version identity, source licensing, bundled dependencies, and authority/side-effect findings.
5. Keep repository-maintenance instructions under `engine/skills/` out of user-facing counts.
6. Treat `INDEX.md` and `index.json` as static snapshots, not generated build artifacts.
7. Create fitted user skills in the user's host or as portable handoff, not by writing into `skillz` during normal use.
8. A returning user may correctly conclude `NO CHANGE NEEDED`.
9. Preserve honest not-run, blocked, rejected, and retired states.
10. Close completed lifecycle objects instead of creating perpetual issue and PR debt.

## Steady state

When admitted-source static coverage is current, public accounting agrees, actionable lifecycle debt is closed, and high-salience discovery omissions are resolved, maintenance shifts toward source freshness, omission detection, candidate evaluation, and selective authorized external evidence. New source discoveries need not reopen a fictional core-implementation project.
